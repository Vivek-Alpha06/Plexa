⚠️ The Problem

$500B+ In Informal Savings Plagued by Fraud & Default

1.4 Billion unbanked adults rely on informal peer circles (Chit Funds, Tandas, Susu), facing massive systemic risks:

🚨 Organizer Embezzlement

Centralized treasurers frequently abscond or misappropriate pooled community funds with zero recourse.

💸 Free-Rider Defaulters

Early winners take the pooled lump-sum and stop contributing to subsequent rounds, victimizing later members.

📉 Zero Credit History

Decades of disciplined participation generate zero verifiable credit scoring for formal financial inclusion.

📓 Opaque Paper Bookkeeping

Physical ledgers lead to accounting errors, delayed disbursements, and unverifiable dispute settlements.

💡 The Solution

Trustless, Mathematical ROSCA on Soroban

Plexa replaces untrusted middlemen with immutable, audited Soroban smart contracts on Stellar.

🛡️ 100% Collateral Escrow

Members deposit non-custodial collateral prior to joining. Defaulters are covered automatically from escrow.

⚡ Open Discount Auction

Members bid a discount to receive funds early. Highest discount wins; the discount splits as dividends among members!

🤖 Autonomous DEX Liquidation

Cross-asset collateral (XLM for USDC pots) is auto-liquidated via Soroswap DEX Router and Reflector Oracles.

📜 On-Chain Reputation

Every cleanly-completed cycle builds portable, verifiable on-chain credit scores without centralized KYC.
